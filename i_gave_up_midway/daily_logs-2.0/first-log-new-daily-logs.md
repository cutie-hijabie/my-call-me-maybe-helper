# First Log in the New Daily Logs

This is the first log in my new daily-log folder.

The day count is **not restarting**. My previous logs are still part of the same Call Me Maybe journey, so the actual work continues from Day 12 onward.

What is restarting is the way I organize and document the project.

I reached a point where the old way of working was frustrating me. I could make progress and fix individual problems, but I kept losing the bigger picture. I wanted a clearer answer to questions like:

- What am I actually trying to make work right now?
- Which parts already exist and can be reused?
- What does this piece connect to?
- Is this a bug, or is this simply something I have not implemented yet?
- What result should I be able to see before moving on?

From this point forward, these logs will follow the project as I work through those questions more deliberately.

I am not deleting or rewriting the old logs. They show the real first half of the process and I want to keep that.

These new logs are the continuation after I realized I needed a different workflow to understand the project better and get clearer results from my work.

The first thing I did after changing the workflow was connect the existing pieces into a real end-to-end run. That immediately exposed performance problems and constraint bugs, but it also made those problems much easier to understand because I knew exactly what capability I was trying to establish.

That is what I want these logs to capture from now on: not a fake perfect path, but a clearer one.
